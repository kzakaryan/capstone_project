Capstone Project Use Cases

Students
As a student, I want to view available courses for the upcoming semester.
As a student, I want to register for a course to complete my academic requirements.
As a student, I want to withdraw from a course before the deadline.
As a student, I want to view my schedule to manage my time.
As a student, I want to view my grades for completed courses.

Instructors
As an instructor, I want to view the list of courses I’m teaching.
As an instructor, I want to see a roster of enrolled students for each course.
As an instructor, I want to record and submit grades for students in my courses.
As an instructor, I want to communicate with students about course-related updates.

Administrators
As an administrator, I want to add new courses and assign instructors to them.
As an administrator, I want to manage student and instructor accounts.
As an administrator, I want to set deadlines for registration and withdrawals.
As an administrator, I want to generate reports on course enrollments and grades.
